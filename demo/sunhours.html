03 / ABOUT THE METHOD

Real computation.
Not an AI-generated heatmap.

This browser implementation ports the sun-position equations and sampling logic from A001_sun_hours.py. It shoots rays toward the sun from sampled roof, facade and ground points, and counts unblocked direct-sun intervals.

Current scope: box-shaped massing presets, local dates, fixed sample intervals and simplified geometry. It is not a replacement for the full Rhino tool, detailed daylight simulation, annual radiation or climate-based compliance studies.

Results are numerical estimates for design exploration. Accuracy depends on geometry detail, sample resolution, orientation and weather-independent direct sun assumptions.

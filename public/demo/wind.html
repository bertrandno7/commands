03 / ABOUT THE MODEL

Flow physics,
not a generated picture.

The solver is adapted from your W001_wind_screening.py: a D2Q9 lattice-Boltzmann model with obstacle bounce-back and Smagorinsky-style local viscosity. It samples a horizontal plane through the building footprints and calculates a relative mean velocity field.

Important: the browser edition uses a smaller domain, fewer steps, and simplified inflow/outflow conditions for speed. It is suitable for early qualitative option comparison, not calibrated wind predictions. Geometry height is only used to determine whether a building intersects the 1.5 m slice.

This is not 3D CFD, does not account for downdrafts/downwash, vertical turbulence, weather statistics, or terrain roughness, and must not be treated as a NEN 8100 wind-comfort assessment. Your W002 command handles additional climate/sector screening separately.
